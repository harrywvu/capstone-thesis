# LiPAD Data-Request Status

Updated 2026-10-16 from the team's report of a LiPAD Support reply, its attached coverage screenshot, and the linked NDA. This note records a prospective source and access conditions; it does not record receipt of a LiPAD dataset.

## Confirmed study area

The team confirmed **Laoag City, Ilocos Norte** as the study area on 2026-10-16. Batac City was an earlier candidate and is no longer part of the active data request. Barangays 38-A, 38-B, and 39 were selected for the SFINCS technical pilot; final evaluation coverage, operating organization, and deployment arrangement remain unresolved. The Laoag and Batac area-of-interest ZIPs in `letters/lipad-aoi/` document the earlier inquiry; their presence does not change the selected study area.

## Provider response and evidence boundary

- UP Training Center for Applied Geodesy and Photogrammetry (UP TCAGP), through LiPAD, replied that its Data Center currently has **DTM, DSM, and orthophotos** available for distribution corresponding to the request. LAZ and SAR availability depends on area coverage. LiPAD supplied a coverage screenshot, but it has no legend or dataset-specific metadata sufficient to establish the exact Laoag DTM footprint or resolution.
- The team requested a **bare-earth DTM** and **Flood Hazard Maps (FHM)**. The reply did not explicitly confirm FHM availability. Ask LiPAD whether FHM for Laoag can be supplied separately.
- LiPAD is a **confirmed prospective DTM provider/contact**, not yet the source of an acquired or adopted OverFlow research dataset. The NDA must be completed, signed, and notarized before LiPAD processes the request further. No delivered LiPAD DTM, final coverage, native resolution, acquisition date, vertical accuracy, reference systems, or use rights have been reported to this knowledge base.
- The team now assumes access to a suitable finer DTM for **planning the SFINCS evaluation**. This does not change LiPAD's actual delivery, quality, or NDA status recorded above.
- The linked NDA limits use to a stated purpose and restricts sharing of confidential data and derivative works. Before signing, clarify who the legal recipient/signatory should be and whether maps or results derived from the data may be shown to advisers/panelists or included in a thesis, defense, or prototype. Do not infer publication rights from the provider's availability reply.
