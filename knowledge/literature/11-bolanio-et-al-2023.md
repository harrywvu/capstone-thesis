## Study Purpose and Context

The study evaluates existing evacuation centers, maps potential shelter sites, and develops a web mapping application for the local government of Las Nieves, Agusan del Norte.

## Data and Methods

- GIS weighted-overlay analysis based on multicriteria decision-making.
- Criteria covering slope, elevation, land cover, roads, water sources, electrical supply, and fuel-station proximity.
- Comparison of 11 existing evacuation centers with the modeled 100-year flood extent.
- Web application for evacuation-center location, status, capacity, and related spatial information.

## Main Results

- 85.1% of the municipality was moderately suitable, 0.3% highly suitable, and 14.6% low in suitability.
- Ten existing centers were moderately suitable.
- The Maningalao Evacuation Center was unsuitable because it was affected by the 100-year flood scenario.

## Relevance and Limits for OverFlow

This is a directly relevant Philippine example of an LGU-oriented geospatial application and shows that listed centers should be checked for flood exposure. The suitability surface depends on selected criteria and weights. It does not simulate route congestion, population assignment, resource distribution, or repeated operational scenarios.

## Safe Uses in the Manuscript

- Support local web-based access to evacuation-center and flood information.
- Support screening existing centers for hazard exposure and spatial suitability.
- Provide a Philippine comparison for the proposed planner-facing interface.

## Do Not Infer

- That every moderately suitable area contains a usable shelter building.
- That the Las Nieves weights or results apply to the eventual OverFlow locality.
- That web presentation alone establishes operational effectiveness.
