# Abuzo et al. (2024): Vulnerable Routes in Cagayan de Oro

## Citation

A. Abuzo, A. Fillone, J. Vallente, Jr., A. Cahulogan, J. M. Osia, and G. Sulay, “Evaluation of vulnerable routes and simulation under normal and flood conditions in Cagayan De Oro City, Philippines,” *Journal of the Eastern Asia Society for Transportation Studies*, vol. 15, pp. 1805–1823, 2024. DOI: [10.11175/easts.15.1805](https://doi.org/10.11175/easts.15.1805).

BibLaTeX key: `abuzo2024`.

## Material Reviewed

- Open-access publisher article metadata and abstract, with accessible indexed method and result material.

## Study Purpose and Context

The study evaluates vulnerable urban routes and changes in traffic conditions when flooding affects Cagayan de Oro City's road network.

## Data and Methods

- Baseline city, road, and traffic data.
- Travel-condition and perception profiles for normal and storm conditions.
- Spatial and traffic-flow simulation.
- Mapping of vulnerable roads and development of mitigation strategies.

## Main Results

- The comparison identified vulnerable routes and changes between ordinary and flooded network conditions.
- A reported alternative route, ML2, had floodwater affecting 60% of its links.
- The analysis produced maps and road-management measures for mitigation planning.

## Relevance and Limits for OverFlow

The Philippine case supports comparing the normal network with a flood-constrained network and evaluating alternate routes as connected links. Its local traffic and flood results cannot be transferred to another city.

## Safe Uses in the Manuscript

- Support baseline-versus-flood scenario comparison.
- Support reporting the share or identity of route links affected by flooding.
- Support evaluation with local road and travel-condition data.

## Do Not Infer

- That all Philippine cities have the same route vulnerabilities.
- That a route with fewer affected links is automatically usable.
- That OverFlow already contains a calibrated traffic-flow model.
