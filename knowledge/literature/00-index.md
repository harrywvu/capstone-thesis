# Literature Evidence Index

## Purpose

This directory stores working evidence notes for the scholarly literature and official technical material used in the OverFlow manuscript. It is an internal research aid: the original publications remain authoritative, and all manuscript citations must use the verified entries in `latex-template-ccis-paper-v2/references/bibliography.bib`.

## Evidence Rules

- Separate what a source demonstrates from what OverFlow proposes.
- Do not transfer performance results, parameters, or validation from another study area to OverFlow.
- Treat D8 as support for flow direction and contributing-area computation, not as evidence that a rainfall overlay produces calibrated flood depth.
- Treat computed routes as planning options unless behavior data and validation support predictive claims.
- Use the recent studies for current methodological context. Retain older works only for foundational methods, historically relevant systems, or locally relevant evidence.
- Record the level of access. Abstract-only notes cannot support details that appear only in a full method or results section.
