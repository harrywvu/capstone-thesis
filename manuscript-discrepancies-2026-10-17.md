# Manuscript–Knowledge Base Resolution Tracker

Updated 2026-10-17 after the team's annotated review of the initial discrepancy audit. This tracker records what changed in the BSIT manuscript and canonical knowledge base. “Resolved” means the wording and documented project decision now agree; it does **not** mean data have been delivered or a model has been validated. The [capstone definition](knowledge/00-capstone-definition.md) and [current decisions](knowledge/decisions.md) govern active scope. The current [manuscript PDF](latex-template-ccis-paper-v2/main.pdf) was rebuilt after the text changes.
