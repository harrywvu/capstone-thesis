We are undergraduate students from the BS Information Technology program at Mariano Marcos State University, currently working on our capstone project entitled **"OverFlow: A 2D Simulation-Based Decision Support System for Flood Evacuation Planning."**

The system is designed to help local disaster planners visualize flood scenarios and evaluate evacuation options. It computes flood-affected areas based on terrain data and rainfall conditions, then analyzes feasible evacuation routes, shelter allocations, estimated clearance times, and resource distribution.

To develop and validate the system, we respectfully request access to the following data from your office:

1. **Evacuation center information** — names, locations (latitude/longitude), barangay assignments, maximum capacity, current or estimated occupancy, accessibility status, and available facilities.
2. **Historical flood data** — records of past flood events in [CITY NAME], including affected areas, approximate flood extent, dates, and duration.
3. **Flood-prone area mapping** — any existing maps or records identifying areas commonly affected by flooding.
4. **Affected population estimates** — estimated number of residents per barangay who may require evacuation during flood events.
5. **Available rescue resources** — inventory of vehicles, equipment, and personnel available for evacuation operations.

The data collected will be used solely for the development and evaluation of the OverFlow system. We will ensure that all information is handled responsibly and will not be shared or published without proper authorization.

We are willing to coordinate with your office at your earliest convenience and can provide any additional documentation required. We would also welcome the opportunity to present our project and discuss how the system may be useful for your office's preparedness planning.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
