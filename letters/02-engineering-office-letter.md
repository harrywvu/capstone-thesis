We are undergraduate students from the BS Information Technology program at Mariano Marcos State University, currently working on our capstone project entitled **"OverFlow: A 2D Simulation-Based Decision Support System for Flood Evacuation Planning."**

The system is designed to help local disaster planners visualize flood scenarios and evaluate evacuation options. It uses elevation and terrain data to compute flood-affected areas, then analyzes evacuation routes and shelter allocations based on road network conditions.

To develop the geographic and infrastructure components of the system, we respectfully request access to the following data from your office:

1. **Road network data** — road locations, geometries, names, classifications, intersections, and bridge locations within [CITY NAME].
2. **Drainage infrastructure data** — locations and geometries of drainage canals, waterways, culverts, and flood-control structures within [CITY NAME].
3. **Known flood-prone roads** — any records or mapping of roads that are commonly affected by flooding.
4. **Road accessibility information** — any data on road conditions or closures during flood events, if available.
5. **Elevation or terrain data** — if your office maintains any digital elevation models, topographic maps, or contour data for [CITY NAME].

The data collected will be used solely for the development and evaluation of the OverFlow system. We will ensure that all information is handled responsibly and will not be shared or published without proper authorization.
