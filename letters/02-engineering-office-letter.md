We are undergraduate students from the BS Information Technology program at Mariano Marcos State University, currently working on our capstone project entitled **"OverFlow: Flood Simulation and Satellite-Derived Flood Information for Scenario-Based Evacuation Planning in Laoag City."**

The planned 3D geospatial system is intended to help local disaster planners visualize flood scenarios and evaluate evacuation options. SFINCS is the selected flood-scenario method; its local terrain and water-boundary setup and flood maps still require evaluation. Road data will support evacuation-route and center-allocation analysis. Pipes and canals will serve as reference layers under the current scope, while river/channel and downstream-boundary information is needed for the flood model.

To develop the geographic and infrastructure components of the system, we respectfully request access to the following data from your office:

1. **Road network data** — road locations, geometries, names, classifications, intersections, and bridge locations within [CITY NAME].
2. **Drainage infrastructure data** — locations and geometries of drainage canals, waterways, culverts, and flood-control structures within [CITY NAME].
3. **Known flood-prone roads** — any records or mapping of roads that are commonly affected by flooding.
4. **Road accessibility information** — any data on road conditions or closures during flood events, if available.
5. **Elevation or terrain data** — if your office maintains any digital elevation models, topographic maps, or contour data for [CITY NAME].
6. **River and boundary data** — channel geometry and elevations, downstream water-level or discharge records, and known inflow or outflow locations relevant to [CITY NAME], if held by your office.

The data collected will be used solely for the development and evaluation of the OverFlow system. We will ensure that all information is handled responsibly and will not be shared or published without proper authorization.

We are willing to coordinate with your office at your earliest convenience and can provide any additional documentation required.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
